Ferrun v0.0.1

Downloads are available for Linux ARM64, Windows ARM64, and Apple silicon Macs at https://ferrun-io.github.io/. Download the bundle for your computer and its checksum.

All three downloads passed the GitHub Actions compatibility suite on their matching platforms. The checks covered checkout, actions, Python setup, cache and artifact round trips, job outputs, failures, long-running jobs, cancellation, timeouts, and summary reporting. Fresh installations also passed checksum and manifest checks, prerequisite and credential failure checks, and executor restart checks.

The programs are unchanged from the original v0.0.1 build. The licensed archives correct the Ferrun license notice; third-party notices remain included. Ferrun is proprietary software.

Installation: https://ferrun-io.github.io/install.html
